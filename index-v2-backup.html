I'm Jarrett, your local Las Vegas Cinematic Web Designer. I went through dentalsolutionslv.com the way a Summerlin patient looking for a cosmetic dentist would: find you, check the reviews, call. What I found

01

The page is heavy on a slow-phone simulation.

On two Lighthouse runs the performance score was 34 and 37, total blocking time was 1,400 and 1,030 milliseconds, and Largest Contentful Paint came at 13.6 and 13.3 seconds. The page makes 115 requests and has 1,759 elements, and third-party code blocked the main thread for 870 milliseconds. File size is modest at 1,776 KiB, so the cost is the amount of code. It is a lab simulation.

02

The header film showed a security message in my test browser.

At desktop width, the Vimeo player behind your headline (Sds-Office-Walkthrough-Edit-3) showed “We couldn’t verify the security of your connection” in three screenshots over 7.5 seconds. That may be a test-browser effect, and I couldn't check a regular browser. At phone width the player did not load at all.

03

Best practices scored 57.

Lighthouse flags 8 third-party cookies, 2 deprecated browser features, and a 401 error from the film player in the browser console. These are lab flags worth a look by your site builder.

04

One image is an iStock stock photo, and the footer says 2025.

The home page loads iStock-2138919283.2506260920490.jpg from your own uploads, which is a stock photo. The footer line reads “© 2025 All Rights Reserved”.

05

Links are hard to tell apart.

Lighthouse lists 106 links inside text that are not distinguished from the text around them, and 4 links that say only “Learn More” or “Read More”, which tell a screen reader user nothing about where they go.

What's working

Your title and description are set and carry your phone number. The home page has Dentist and LocalBusiness markup with opening hours, and FAQPage markup with 20 questions. There is a 70 KB llms.txt file, which is unusual, and a sitemap index. Your number, (702) 341-9160, is a tap-to-call link, and all 67 images have alt text. The HTTPS certificate is valid until January 14, 2027. Lighthouse lab scores: accessibility 94, SEO 92.

Unmeasured

Rankings, AI assistant answers, visitor numbers and your Google listing. Whether the header film works in a regular browser. A real phone: I tested the page width in a desktop browser only. Whether the “Priya” case on your Smile Makeover page is a real patient or an illustration, which is for you to say.
